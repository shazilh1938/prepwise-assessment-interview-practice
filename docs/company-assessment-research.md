# Company-Assessment Research Standard

Prepwise uses employer and assessment names descriptively to help candidates find relevant preparation. It does not claim access to confidential employer materials.

## Evidence Priority

When reviewing whether a company or programme should appear in a public guide, Prepwise prioritises:

1. current official employer recruitment pages;
2. current official assessment-provider documentation;
3. invitation details voluntarily described without sharing confidential content;
4. multiple consistent candidate reports;
5. older or single-source reports, clearly labelled as uncertain.

Third-party opportunity feeds can identify a programme worth checking, but they are not sufficient on their own for a public claim about the assessment format.

## Publication Rules

- State the country, role or programme year when it affects the process.
- Use cautious wording when formats can vary.
- Separate confirmed evidence from candidate-reported signals.
- Avoid reproducing proprietary questions, screenshots or invitation content.
- Update or remove claims when stronger evidence contradicts them.
- Link candidates to the official employer source whenever possible.

## Reporting an Update

A useful correction includes:

- employer and programme name;
- country and application year;
- the specific statement that may be outdated;
- a public official source, where available;
- the date the source was checked.

Do not include personal data, application IDs, private recruiter contact details or material covered by a confidentiality agreement.
