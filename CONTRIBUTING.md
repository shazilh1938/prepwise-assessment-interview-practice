# Contributing to Prepwise Public Resources

Thank you for helping make candidate preparation more accurate.

## Useful Contributions

- correcting a broken or outdated public link;
- reporting a changed employer recruitment process;
- suggesting a missing clarification in a preparation guide;
- describing a product accessibility or usability issue;
- proposing a guide for a graduate programme or assessment format.

## Evidence Standard

For company-assessment reports, include:

- company or programme;
- role, country and application year where relevant;
- the exact statement that needs review;
- a current official public source where one exists;
- whether the information is confirmed or candidate-reported.

A third-party vacancy aggregator can start a review, but Prepwise verifies public claims through direct official sources before publication.

## Do Not Submit

- real assessment questions copied from a live test;
- screenshots of a confidential assessment;
- invitation links, application IDs or recruiter contact details;
- CVs, transcripts or other personal data;
- credentials or security-sensitive information;
- content covered by a confidentiality agreement.

## Product Suggestions

Describe the candidate problem first:

1. What were you trying to prepare for?
2. What did you expect Prepwise to do?
3. What happened instead?
4. What change would make the next practice session more useful?

Prepwise may close requests that would encourage misleading scores, unsupported employer claims or collection of unnecessary personal data.
